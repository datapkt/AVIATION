Drone Cinematography

In short: we shoot 4K drone video and high-resolution aerial photographs across India, covering events, tourism promotion and real estate advertising, among other work. Our drone pilots plan and fly each shoot, and our video editors edit and grade the footage in-house. We also produce aerial panoramas and video tours of facilities, so businesses can show their sites and assets from above.

Aerial photography

Our drone cinematographers produce high-resolution aerial photographs of properties and assets, from residential properties to resort hotels. Each shoot is planned around the client's brief, so the angles, timing and framing match what the images need to show.

Whether the images are for marketing, documentation or personal use, we frame each shot to show the property clearly and in its setting.

Aerial videography

We shoot 4K drone video to each client's requirements using professional cinematography drones. Our video editors then cut and grade the clips into finished videos that help businesses reach more customers.

Our Features

Fast Data Delivery

Our in-house GIS team processes and analyses drone survey data quickly, and we often deliver ahead of schedule.

Survey-Grade Accuracy

Every survey is tied to DGPS ground control points, so measurements and volumes can be relied on.

Team of Experts

Our GIS engineers, drone pilots and software engineers work round the clock to keep projects on schedule.

Advanced Equipment

We fly both DJI models and custom-built drones fitted with high-resolution sensors, chosen to suit each survey.
