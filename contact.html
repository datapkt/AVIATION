What to include in your enquiry

Send the site location as a KML/KMZ file or chainage, the area or corridor length, the outputs you need, the accuracy your design needs, and your deadline, including whether the survey is one-off or repeated. A short message is enough to start, and with these details we can suggest the right survey.

If you can, include:

  • The site as a KML/KMZ file, or the chainage and road or rail line name.
  • Area or corridor length, and any parts under tree cover or in hills.
  • The outputs you need: orthomosaic, DTM, contours, CAD drawings, point cloud or video.
  • The accuracy your design needs, or the specification you are working to.
  • Your deadline, and whether the survey is one-off or repeated (for example monthly).

Not sure which method or output you need? Tell us what the data is for (a DPR, an Engineering Scale Plan, progress monitoring, land acquisition, a thermal inspection) and we will suggest the right survey. Our guides on deliverables and cost explain the main choices.
