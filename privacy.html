Privacy and cookies

What this website collects, why, and the choices you have.

Enquiries

When you send an enquiry, the details you type (name, email, phone, company and message) reach our team by email so we can reply. We do not sell or share them for marketing.

Analytics cookies

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful, such as which services people read about and how they found us. It does not show us who you are. If you choose Accept, we may also use Microsoft Clarity to see anonymised page interactions, which helps us fix confusing pages. We do not use advertising cookies.

Your choice

Use the Accept or Decline buttons in the cookie notice. If you decline, analytics cookies are switched off on this browser. To change your choice later, clear this site's data in your browser and the notice will appear again.

WhatsApp and phone

The WhatsApp button opens a chat with +91 84485 59100 in WhatsApp. We count clicks on it, on phone numbers and on email links, without recording what you write.
