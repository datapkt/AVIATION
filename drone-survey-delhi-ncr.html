Clients in Delhi NCR

Our clients here include Delhi Metro Rail Corporation (DMRC), Delhi State Industrial and Infrastructure Development Corporation (DSIIDC), Municipal Corporation of Gurugram (MCG), Municipal Corporation Manesar and Faridabad Metropolitan Development Authority (FMDA). See the full client list.

What we survey in Delhi NCR

  • Metro and railway corridors: base surveys, progress monitoring and corridor mapping
  • Industrial estates and municipal areas: base maps, property and utility mapping
  • Highways and expressways around the capital: DPR surveys and monthly highway drone monitoring
  • Solar rooftops and plants: drone thermography

Working in Delhi NCR

Much of Delhi falls in yellow or red zones on the Digital Sky airspace map, around the airport and central government areas. We check every site on the map and plan permissions before the flight date.

Every survey is tied to DGPS ground control and checked against independent checkpoints, and delivered as drawings, GIS layers and reports in your coordinate system. Read what each deliverable is and what decides the cost.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have a team in Gurugram?

Yes. Our office is at 434, 4th Floor, JMD Megapolis, Sohna Road, Sector 48, Gurugram 122018, and our survey teams work across Delhi NCR from there.

Can drones fly anywhere in Delhi?

No. Large parts of Delhi are in yellow or red zones on the Digital Sky airspace map and need prior permission. We check each site and apply before flying.

Which NCR clients have you worked for?

Clients in the region include DMRC, DSIIDC, the Municipal Corporation of Gurugram, Municipal Corporation Manesar and FMDA.
