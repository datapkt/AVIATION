Drone survey vs DGPS and total station: which should you use?

When a drone survey beats DGPS and total station, when it does not, and why most infrastructure surveys combine all three. Coverage, accuracy, safety and deliverables compared.

By DATA PKT Infra & Geomatics · Published 27 October 2026

Short answer: they do different jobs. DGPS gives a few points of very high accuracy, a total station measures detail where satellites or drones cannot reach, and a drone captures everything across the whole site. Most infrastructure surveys use all three: DGPS for control, a drone for coverage and a total station for hidden detail.

What each method does best

The combined workflow we use

  1. DGPS fixes ground control points and independent checkpoints in the project coordinate system.
  2. The drone flies the whole site or corridor, with RTK or PPK where available.
  3. Total station or DGPS picks up what the drone cannot see: ground under dense trees, inside culverts, structure levels.
  4. Processing merges everything into one terrain model and drawing set, and the checkpoints prove the final accuracy.

When a ground-only survey still makes sense

  • Very small plots, where flight planning and processing outweigh the fieldwork saved
  • Sites under continuous dense canopy, if LiDAR is not in budget
  • Indoor, underground or under-bridge detail
  • Red-zone airspace where flight permission is not available in time

Frequently asked questions

Does a drone survey replace DGPS?

No. DGPS measures the ground control points and checkpoints that make a drone survey accurate. The drone captures the whole area; DGPS ties it to real coordinates and proves the accuracy.

When is a total station still needed?

Under dense trees, inside structures, for precise levels of bridge bearings, drains and rail tops, and wherever a drone cannot fly or see.

Is a drone survey accepted for DPRs?

Drone surveys tied to DGPS control are widely used for DPR topography in India, with ground survey completing the details a drone cannot capture.
