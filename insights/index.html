Practical guides to drone survey, mapping and GIS

Plain answers to the questions engineers, project managers and plant owners ask us about drone surveys, LiDAR, thermography, costs and deliverables.

These guides come from our day-to-day survey work on highways, railways, power lines and water projects. Each one answers a question clients ask before a survey starts: which method to use, how accurate the result will be, what the deliverables are, what it costs and what to put in the scope. New to drone surveys? Begin with the three guides below. How we write these guides.
