Services

Drone survey, LiDAR, GIS and inspection services

Every DATA PKT service on one page: survey and mapping, infrastructure survey and design, inspection, and the sectors we work in.

In short: DATA PKT surveys, maps and designs for infrastructure across India. Our 20+ drone pilots fly photogrammetry, LiDAR and thermal surveys tied to DGPS ground control, and our in-house GIS team turns the data into drawings and models. We have collected 3 lakh+ km of highway data in six years and are empanelled with NHAI in three zones.
