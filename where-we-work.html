Our GIS team also offers remote LiDAR, mapping and drafting work to clients outside India; all our projects so far have been in India.

What we need to plan a survey anywhere in India

Wherever the site is, the same few details let us plan the flights, ground control and team size, and give you a firm quote:

  • The site as a KML/KMZ file, or the chainage and road or rail line name.
  • Area or corridor length, and any parts under tree cover or in hills.
  • The outputs you need: orthomosaic, DTM, contours, CAD drawings, point cloud or video.
  • The accuracy your design needs, or the specification you are working to.
  • Your deadline, and whether the survey is one-off or repeated (for example monthly).

For hill and forest corridors we usually recommend LiDAR, because it reaches the ground through gaps in vegetation.

Send us the site KML or chainage and the outputs you need. We reply with a scope and quote.
