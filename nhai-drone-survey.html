Monthly drone surveys for NHAI highway projects

DATA PKT is empanelled with the National Highways Authority of India in three zones for monthly drone survey work. We fly each package along the same path every month and deliver the comparative video and survey records the project needs.

ForNHAI PIUs, contractors, concessionaires

Short answer: NHAI requires a monthly drone video of every national highway project, comparing this month with last month, uploaded to its Data Lake portal. DATA PKT flies these surveys for NHAI project units and for contractors, as an agency empanelled in three NHAI zones.

What NHAI requires

In 2021 NHAI made drone video recording mandatory for national highway projects, every month, at every stage: development, construction, and operation and maintenance (Mobility Outlook, June 2021).

  • Comparative video: the current month is shown against the previous month, so progress and changes are clear.
  • Data Lake: videos are uploaded to NHAI's Data Lake portal and stored permanently.
  • Evidence: the videos can be used in disputes before courts and arbitration tribunals.
  • Review: supervision consultants use them in the digital monthly progress report, and NHAI officers use them during site inspections.

How a monthly cycle runs

  1. Package plan: we fix the chainages, the flight path and the camera settings for each package once, so every month is flown the same way.
  2. Permissions: stretches in yellow or red zones on the Digital Sky airspace map are cleared before the flight date.
  3. Flight: DATA PKT pilots fly the full length of the package on the agreed date.
  4. Processing: our team edits the comparative video and, where the scope includes it, the orthomosaic and progress measurements.
  5. Hand-over: files are delivered for the monthly progress report and the Data Lake upload.

Frequently asked questions

Are monthly drone surveys mandatory on NHAI projects?

Yes. Since 2021 NHAI has required monthly drone video recording of national highway projects across development, construction, operation and maintenance, uploaded to its Data Lake portal as a comparison of the current and previous month.

Who records the monthly drone video?

On projects under construction, contractors and concessionaires record it in the presence of the supervision team. NHAI's own project units arrange surveys before construction starts, after completion and on projects it operates and maintains. DATA PKT works for both.

Is DATA PKT empanelled with NHAI?

Yes. DATA PKT is empanelled with NHAI in three zones for monthly drone survey work, and has flown highway drone surveys since October 2020.

Can a contractor or concessionaire hire DATA PKT for the monthly video?

Yes. We fly the package every month along the same path and hand over the comparative video and survey outputs in the form the project needs for its monthly reporting.

What if the highway passes through a yellow or red airspace zone?

The Digital Sky airspace map decides this. Green zones need no flight permission; yellow and red zones need prior permission, which we plan for before the monthly cycle starts.
