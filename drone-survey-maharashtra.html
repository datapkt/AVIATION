What to plan for in Maharashtra

In Maharashtra, plan around the monsoon: it reaches Mumbai around 11 June and withdraws around 8 October, so base surveys suit October to May; slopes and drains are flown before and after the rains. Ghat and coastal sections under tree cover are surveyed with drone LiDAR, and railway sites need the division’s permission and a block or flying slot.

Monsoon. On India Meteorological Department normals the monsoon reaches Mumbai around 11 June and withdraws around 8 October; for Pune the dates are around 10 June and 11 October, and for Nagpur around 15 June and 6 October (IMD). Cloud and rain on the coast and the ghats cut flying days in those months, so base surveys suit October to May. Slopes and drains are the exception: fly them before and after the rains so the change can be measured.

Terrain. The state runs from the Mumbai region and the Konkan coast, over the Western Ghats, to the Deccan plateau. The Geological Survey of India maps about 29,000 sq km of Maharashtra as landslide-prone (Ministry of Earth Sciences, 2025). On ghat and coastal sections, tree cover and steep cuttings hide the ground from a camera, which is where drone LiDAR is used.

Common projects. Suburban and mainline railway yards and corridors; metro corridors; the Samruddhi Mahamarg (Nagpur–Mumbai) expressway (Deccan Herald); and the Mumbai–Ahmedabad high-speed rail line, which includes a 21 km tunnel between Bandra-Kurla Complex and Shilphata (NHSRCL).

Drone permissions. Under the Drone Rules, 2021, the Ministry of Civil Aviation’s airspace map, published on DGCA’s Digital Sky platform, divides India into green, yellow and red zones. Green zones need no flight permission. Yellow zones, which include the ground up from 5 to 8 km of an operational airport perimeter and the airspace above 200 feet from 8 to 12 km, need air traffic control permission. Red zones need Central Government permission (PIB, 2021). Near airports, ports and defence areas the permission usually sets the start date. Railway sites also need the division’s permission and a block or flying slot (railway drone guide).

Frequently asked questions

Which railway clients have you worked for in Maharashtra?

Our clients include Mumbai Railway Vikas Corporation (MRVC), Central Railway and Konkan Railway.

Can you prepare Engineering Scale Plans for Mumbai suburban yards?

Yes. We prepare ESPs and yard plans from drone surveys tied to DGPS control, with critical details verified on the ground.

How do drones help on busy suburban lines?

They capture a whole yard or section in a short window, so staff spend far less time on or beside running lines.

When should a drone survey be flown in Maharashtra?

For base mapping, October to May. The India Meteorological Department’s normal monsoon dates for Mumbai run from about 11 June to 8 October, and cloud and rain on the coast and the ghats cut flying days in that period. For slopes and drains, we recommend one flight before the monsoon and one after, so the change can be measured.

Do you need LiDAR for ghat sections in Maharashtra?

Usually, yes. On ghat sections, trees and steep cuttings hide the ground from a camera, so photogrammetry alone gives the canopy, not the formation. Drone LiDAR reaches the ground between the vegetation. Where a slope is moving, the LiDAR model goes into a slope stability investigation with seismic refraction and geological mapping.
