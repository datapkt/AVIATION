Case studies

Survey, mapping and design projects for India's infrastructure

Real projects, described plainly: what the client needed, how we used drones and DGPS to do it, and what we delivered or are delivering.

3 lakh+ kmHighway data captured in six years for authorities and private contractors
About 5 sq km a daySurvey data captured by one drone team
60+ yard plansYard plans (ESPs) and drainage plans submitted, plus drainage design for 32 yards

These six projects show how we survey and design infrastructure in India: monthly drone monitoring of highways, yard and drainage plans for railway divisions, a 220/132 kV substation and its lines, a canal-fed groundwater recharge system, a slope stability investigation of a hill road, and quality control of drone thermography data from a solar plant. Each case study sets out the need, the methods, the quantities from the project and the deliverables, so you can judge how we would handle a similar site.

Highways · Multiple states

Highway drone monitoring: more than 3 lakh km of data for authorities and contractors

For highway authorities, highway contractors and private developers, DATA PKT sets DGPS control, flies drone photogrammetry and aerial videography every month, and turns the data into analytics on progress and problems on the road.

Surveyed
Highway packages flown along the same path every month, at the development, construction, and operation and maintenance stages
Methods
DGPS ground control, drone photogrammetry, aerial videography, AI analytics
Key figures
3 lakh+ km of highway data in six years; about 35 km a day of imagery and about 50 km a day of video per team; 20+ pilots
Deliverables
Georeferenced orthomosaics, the comparative monthly video, and analytics on rate of completion, encroachments, unauthorised U-turns, potholes and black spots
Read the case study: highway drone monitoring →
Railways · Eastern India, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh

Yard plans and drainage plans for two railway divisions

Station yards surveyed with drones and DGPS for two railway divisions, and turned into yard plans and drainage designs, with critical details checked on the ground.

Surveyed
Station yards, from suburban yards and small wayside stations to main-line junctions
Methods
Drone survey with DGPS control, orthomosaic and terrain model, CAD drafting, field checks
Key figures
60+ yard plans (ESPs) and drainage plans in eastern India (30 yard plans and 31 drainage plans, all submitted); drainage design for 32 yards in Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh; six-month contract periods
Deliverables
Yard plans, drainage plans, and for each drainage yard a survey report, waste-water estimate, drainage slopes and disposal location, manholes and septic tanks, and layout plans
Read the case study: railway yard and drainage plans →
Power transmission · Uttar Pradesh

220/132 kV substation and transmission lines in Uttar Pradesh

Survey and design for a railway traction power project: a new 220/132 kV grid substation, the 220 kV and 132 kV lines that feed it, and location plans along the railway. In progress.

Surveyed
Three alternate routes for each line, the approved routes in detail, the substation site and 19 sites along the railway
Methods
Drone photogrammetry laid over khasra maps, DGPS and total station survey with levelling, boreholes with SPT, soil resistivity tests
Key figures
220 kV D/C (32 m ROW) and 132 kV D/C (25 m ROW) lines; 19 sites: the GSS, 4 traction substations and 14 sectioning and sub-sectioning posts
Deliverables
Comparative feasibility report, longitudinal profiles with tower spotting, tower and crossing schedules, switchyard and building GAD, LT single line diagram, location plans
Read the case study: 220/132 kV substation and transmission lines →
Water and irrigation · Haryana

Groundwater recharge system for Mahendragarh: survey and design of two canal-fed lines

Survey and design of a groundwater recharge system in Mahendragarh district: two lines take water from an existing canal or river to structures that return it to the aquifer. In progress.

Surveyed
Two conveyance corridors, the off-take, crossings and candidate recharge sites such as low ground and village ponds
Methods
Drone mapping tied to DGPS ground control and benchmarks, ground levels with DGPS and total station
Key figures
Two lines of about 45 km and 15 km
Deliverables
Alignment, longitudinal sections, hydraulic sizing, drawings of the off-take, silt chambers, crossings and recharge structures, quantities, and survey data in CAD and GIS formats
Read the case study: groundwater recharge, Mahendragarh →
Landslide and slope stability · Assam

Slope stability investigation of a 66 km road corridor in Assam

For a private client, the road-side slopes of a hill road corridor were investigated with three data sets combined in one ground model, and every stretch was ranked by hazard.

Surveyed
About 66 km of road-side cut and fill slopes, in a ~100 m wide LiDAR corridor
Methods
RTK/PPK drone LiDAR with DGPS control, seismic refraction tomography, engineering geological mapping
Key figures
66 seismic refraction profiles (~1 per km); geological mapping at 7 locations (2.12 km); about 9 weeks, with about 3 weeks in the field
Deliverables
Classified point cloud, DTM and DSM, 0.5 m contours, velocity tomograms, factor of safety in dry, saturated and earthquake conditions, hazard ranking, protection design inputs
Read the case study: slope stability investigation, Assam →
Solar PV · Tamil Nadu

Solar PV thermography in Tamil Nadu: what data QC catches before the report

Drone thermal and RGB data from a utility-scale solar plant, checked against IEC TS 62446-3 capture conditions, overlap and positioning before analysis. In progress.

Surveyed
A solar PV plant of about 340 MWp in 19 blocks, flown by a partner crew to DATA PKT's scope
Methods
Drone thermal (radiometric IR) and RGB acquisition, processing and quality control against IEC TS 62446-3 conditions, overlap and positioning
Key figures
16,548 module structures, 33,096 strings and about 1.03 million modules; flights 12–18 October 2026
Deliverables
QC findings and the areas to be recaptured; the thermography analysis and report are prepared only from data that passes QC
Read the case study: solar PV thermography data QC →

About these case studies

Each case study follows the same structure: what the client needed, what we surveyed and how, the deliverables, and what the data was used for. Together they cover the main kinds of work we do: repeat monitoring of highways, railway yard, drainage and Engineering Scale Plans, survey and design for power transmission, water resources design, slope stability investigation, and solar plant thermography. For the method behind railway plans, see Engineering Scale Plans and DPR surveys.

We describe projects without naming clients or exact sites unless we have permission to, and we never publish contract values or coordinates. The figures on these pages (kilometres captured, plans prepared, line lengths) are the actual quantities from each project.
