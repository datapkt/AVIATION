Highway drone monitoring: more than 3 lakh km of data for authorities and contractors

For highway authorities, highway contractors and private developers, DATA PKT sets DGPS control, flies drone photogrammetry and aerial videography every month, and turns the data into analytics that show progress and problems on the road.

ClientsHighway authorities, highway contractors and private developers
Services usedDrone photogrammetry, aerial videography, DGPS control and analytics
Highway data capturedMore than 3 lakh km in six years, imagery and videography
EmpanelmentThree NHAI zones, the most allowed

In short: every month our pilots fly each highway package along the same path, tied to DGPS ground control. The imagery becomes georeferenced maps (photogrammetry), the video becomes the comparative monthly record, and both feed analytics that measure the rate of completion and flag anomalies on completed highways.

What a drone service provider does

Highway drone programmes separate two roles. The drone service provider (DSP) works in the field and on the data: it flies the highway, captures it and turns it into information. The technology service provider (TSP) runs the technology platform the data goes into. As a DSP, DATA PKT delivers:

  • DGPS ground control, so every map is tied to real coordinates
  • Drone photogrammetry: georeferenced orthomosaics and measurements of each package
  • Aerial videography: the comparative monthly video
  • Analytics on that data, including AI analytics, described below

Why drones are faster

A conventional ground survey with DGPS and total station measures one point at a time, so a team covers a small area each day and a long corridor takes weeks. A DATA PKT drone team captures around 5 sq km of survey data in a day, which is then mapped and analysed in the office. On a linear corridor such as a highway, one team captures about 35 km of drone imagery for photogrammetry, or about 50 km of aerial video, in a day, and DATA PKT has more than 20 drone pilots (a team of about 22) to put in the field. Ground instruments are still used, but only where they matter most: for the control points that fix the map to real coordinates, for checkpoints that prove the accuracy, and for detail the drone cannot see.

Why highways need a monthly drone record

In 2021 NHAI made drone video recording mandatory for national highway projects, every month, at every stage: development, construction, and operation and maintenance (Mobility Outlook, June 2021). Videos compare the current month with the previous one and can be used as evidence in disputes. Contractors and private developers use the same record for their own progress reports and billing.

How a monthly cycle runs

  1. Package plan: chainages, flight path and camera settings are fixed once, so every month is flown the same way.
  2. Control and permissions: DGPS ground control is set, and stretches in yellow or red airspace zones are cleared before the flight date.
  3. Flight: DATA PKT pilots fly the full length of the package.
  4. Processing and analytics: orthomosaics, the comparative video and the analytics are prepared in the office.
  5. Hand-over: files go to the client's monthly progress report and data portal.

How it started

Our first highway drone work, in October 2020, was aerial imagery, videography and survey for highway projects in Nagaland and Manipur. It grew into NHAI empanelment in three zones and a client base that mixes highway authorities, including NHAI, NHIDCL and the Border Roads Organisation, with more than 80 highway contractors and private developers, among them G R Infraprojects, PNC Infratech, H.G. Frequently asked questions

How many kilometres of highway has DATA PKT flown?

More than 3 lakh km of highway data in the last six years, drone imagery and aerial videography combined, for highway authorities and private contractors. In this financial year (2026-27) we have already captured more than 70,000 km, and the count is still rising.

How fast can a drone team cover a highway?

One DATA PKT drone team captures about 35 km of highway imagery for photogrammetry, or about 50 km of aerial video, in a day. We have more than 20 drone pilots, a team of about 22.

What is a DSP in a highway drone programme?

A drone service provider (DSP) flies the highways, sets DGPS ground control, captures imagery and video, and processes the data. A technology service provider (TSP) runs the technology platform the data feeds into. DATA PKT works as a DSP.

What can the analytics find on a highway?

The rate of completion on projects under construction, and on completed highways: encroachments and broken boundaries, unauthorised U-turns and paths cut by the public, potholes, black spots and accident-prone stretches.

Are monthly drone surveys mandatory on national highway projects?

Yes. Since 2021 NHAI has required monthly drone video recording of national highway projects across development, construction, operation and maintenance, uploaded to its Data Lake portal.

Is DATA PKT empanelled with NHAI?

Yes. DATA PKT is empanelled with NHAI in three zones for monthly drone survey work, and has flown highway drone surveys since October 2020.
