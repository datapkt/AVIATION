Monthly drone monitoring of national highways for NHAI

For the National Highways Authority of India, DATA PKT flies highway packages every month and delivers two of its services: drone photogrammetry and aerial videography. We are empanelled with NHAI in three zones.

ClientNational Highways Authority of India (NHAI) project units
Services usedDrone photogrammetry and aerial videography
EmpanelmentThree NHAI zones, the most allowed
Highway drone work sinceOctober 2020

In short: NHAI requires a monthly drone video of every national highway project, comparing this month with last month. For NHAI's project units, DATA PKT flies each package every month along the same path, and delivers the comparative video (aerial videography) and georeferenced maps and measurements (photogrammetry).

The client's requirement

In 2021 NHAI made drone video recording mandatory for national highway projects, every month, at every stage: development, construction, and operation and maintenance (Mobility Outlook, June 2021).

  • Comparative video: the current month is shown against the previous month, so progress and changes are clear.
  • Data Lake: videos are uploaded to NHAI's Data Lake portal and stored permanently.
  • Evidence: the videos can be used in disputes before courts and arbitration tribunals.
  • Review: supervision consultants use them in the digital monthly progress report, and NHAI officers use them during site inspections.

How a monthly cycle runs

  1. Package plan: we fix the chainages, the flight path and the camera settings for each package once, so every month is flown the same way.
  2. Permissions: stretches in yellow or red zones on the Digital Sky airspace map are cleared before the flight date.
  3. Flight: DATA PKT pilots fly the full length of the package on the agreed date.
  4. Processing: our team edits the comparative video and builds the orthomosaic and progress measurements.
  5. Hand-over: files are delivered for the monthly progress report and the Data Lake upload.

How it started

Our first highway drone work, in October 2020, was aerial imagery, videography and survey for NHIDCL projects in Nagaland and Manipur, around Dimapur and Kohima. That work grew into the monthly NHAI monitoring we run today across three zones. Packages are planned centrally from Gurugram and flown by DATA PKT pilots.

Other clients for this work

Besides NHAI and NHIDCL, the Border Roads Organisation and more than 80 highway and infrastructure contractors use the same services for their projects, among them G R Infraprojects, PNC Infratech, H.G. Infra Engineering, Ceigall India, IRB Infrastructure and AFCONS. See more case studies.

Frequently asked questions

Are monthly drone surveys mandatory on NHAI projects?

Yes. Since 2021 NHAI has required monthly drone video recording of national highway projects across development, construction, operation and maintenance, uploaded to its Data Lake portal as a comparison of the current and previous month.

Which DATA PKT services does this work use?

Two: drone photogrammetry, for georeferenced maps and progress measurements, and aerial videography, for the monthly comparative video.

Is DATA PKT empanelled with NHAI?

Yes. DATA PKT is empanelled with NHAI in three zones for monthly drone survey work, and has flown highway drone surveys since October 2020.

Do you do the same work for contractors?

Yes. Contractors and concessionaires on NHAI projects use the same photogrammetry and aerial videography services for their monthly records.
