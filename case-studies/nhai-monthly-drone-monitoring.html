Drone service provider for NHAI: 43,873 km of highways in one year

DATA PKT is a drone service provider (DSP) for the National Highways Authority of India, empanelled in three zones. We set DGPS control, fly drone photogrammetry and aerial videography every month, and turn the data into analytics that show progress and problems on the road.

In short: every month our pilots fly NHAI's highway packages along the same path, tied to DGPS ground control. The imagery becomes georeferenced maps (photogrammetry), the video becomes the comparative monthly record, and both feed analytics that measure the rate of completion and flag anomalies on completed highways.

What a drone service provider does

NHAI's drone programme separates two roles. The drone service provider (DSP) works in the field and on the data: it flies the highway, captures it and turns it into information. The technology service provider (TSP) runs the technology platform the data goes into. As a DSP, DATA PKT delivers:

  • DGPS ground control, so every map is tied to real coordinates
  • Drone photogrammetry: georeferenced orthomosaics and measurements of each package
  • Aerial videography: the comparative monthly video NHAI requires
  • Analytics on that data, including AI analytics, described below

Why drones are faster

A conventional ground survey with DGPS and total station measures one point at a time, so a team covers a small area each day and a long corridor takes weeks. A DATA PKT drone team captures around 5 sq km of survey data in a day, which is then mapped and analysed in the office. Ground instruments are still used, but only where they matter most: for the control points that fix the map to real coordinates, for checkpoints that prove the accuracy, and for detail the drone cannot see.

The client's requirement

In 2021 NHAI made drone video recording mandatory for national highway projects, every month, at every stage: development, construction, and operation and maintenance (Mobility Outlook, June 2021). Videos compare the current month with the previous one, are uploaded to NHAI's Data Lake portal, and can be used as evidence in disputes.

How a monthly cycle runs

  1. Package plan: chainages, flight path and camera settings are fixed once, so every month is flown the same way.
  2. Control and permissions: DGPS ground control is set, and stretches in yellow or red airspace zones are cleared before the flight date.
  3. Flight: DATA PKT pilots fly the full length of the package.
  4. Processing and analytics: orthomosaics, the comparative video and the analytics are prepared in the office.
  5. Hand-over: files go to the monthly progress report and the Data Lake.

How it started

Our first highway drone work, in October 2020, was aerial imagery, videography and survey for NHIDCL projects in Nagaland and Manipur, around Dimapur and Kohima. It grew into NHAI empanelment in three zones; our current NHAI imagery engagement runs to 2028. Besides NHAI and NHIDCL, the Border Roads Organisation and more than 80 highway contractors use the same services, among them G R Infraprojects, PNC Infratech, H.G. Infra Engineering, Ceigall India, IRB Infrastructure and AFCONS.

Frequently asked questions

What is a DSP in NHAI's drone programme?

A drone service provider (DSP) flies the highways, sets DGPS ground control, captures imagery and video, and processes the data. A technology service provider (TSP) runs the technology platform the data feeds into. DATA PKT works as a DSP for NHAI.

How many kilometres does DATA PKT fly for NHAI?

In FY 2025-26 (April 2025 to March 2026) our pilots flew 43,873 km of national highways for NHAI: 32,227 km of imagery and 11,646 km of videography.

What can the analytics find on a highway?

The rate of completion on projects under construction, and on completed highways: encroachments and broken boundaries, unauthorised U-turns and paths cut by the public, potholes, black spots and accident-prone stretches.

Are monthly drone surveys mandatory on NHAI projects?

Yes. Since 2021 NHAI has required monthly drone video recording of national highway projects across development, construction, operation and maintenance, uploaded to its Data Lake portal.

Is DATA PKT empanelled with NHAI?

Yes. DATA PKT is empanelled with NHAI in three zones for monthly drone survey work, and has flown highway drone surveys since October 2020.
