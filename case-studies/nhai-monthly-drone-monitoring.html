Case study · Highways

Monthly drone monitoring of national highways for NHAI

DATA PKT is empanelled with the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) in three zones, the most allowed, to fly monthly drone surveys of highway packages for project implementation units (PIUs).

ClientNational Highways Authority of India (NHAI) PIUs
EmpanelmentThree NHAI zones
ServiceMonthly drone survey and progress monitoring
Highway drone work sinceOctober 2020

The need

NHAI's project implementation units manage long highway packages under construction and in operation. They need a regular, objective record of each stretch: what has been built since last month, where work has stalled, and the condition of the road and its surroundings. Site visits alone cannot cover every kilometre every month.

What we do

Every month our pilots fly the full length of each assigned package along the same corridor, so each month's survey can be compared directly with the last. Our GIS team processes the flights into georeferenced outputs and the records each PIU needs for its monthly review.

How we run it at scale

Packages are spread across three NHAI zones, so flights are planned centrally from Gurugram and flown by DATA PKT pilots. Each package keeps the same flight plan month to month, which keeps the data consistent and comparable.

Where it started

Our first highway drone work, in October 2020, was aerial imagery, videography and survey for highway projects in Nagaland and Manipur (NHIDCL, around Dimapur and Kohima). That work grew into the monthly NHAI monitoring we run today.
