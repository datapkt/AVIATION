Hyperspectral and Multispectral Surveys

Planning and preparation

Our skilled team collaborates closely with clients to grasp their precise goals and needs for the hyperspectral survey. We conduct detailed site assessments to identify the best flight routes, sensor setups, and parameters for data collection.

During site assessment we look at terrain, environmental conditions and project scope, and use these to set flight paths, sensor configuration and data collection parameters.

Planning each survey around the client's objectives and any regulatory requirements sets the base for reliable data collection and analysis.

Data Acquisition

We fly UAVs carrying high-resolution hyperspectral sensors that capture imagery across many spectral bands. Pilots follow pre-planned flight routes for complete coverage of the survey area.

Planned flight paths and consistent sensor settings give complete, accurate data that can be analysed in detail.

The data collected supports applications including environmental monitoring, agriculture and mineral exploration.

Data Processing and Analysis

We process raw hyperspectral data with specialised software, using spectral unmixing, classification and feature extraction. Outputs are matched to each client's needs and include spectral signatures, vegetation indices, mineral maps and environmental indicators.

These methods let us interpret the data reliably and extract the information relevant to each project.

Spectral signatures characterise materials, vegetation indices show plant health, mineral maps show geological composition and environmental indicators track ecological conditions, giving users in agriculture, environmental monitoring and resource management data they can act on.

Interpretation and Reporting

We interpret the analysed data and turn it into clear findings and recommendations, presented in reports and maps that clients can act on.

Benefits of Our Drone Hyperspectral Survey Service:

  • Precision and Accuracy: Hyperspectral sensors on our UAVs, with careful processing, give precise and accurate results.
  • Efficiency and cost-effectiveness: Drone-based surveys offer a faster and more cost-effective alternative to traditional methods, reducing time and resource requirements.
  • Insights and Intelligence: Detailed spectral information supports analysis and interpretation for a wide range of applications.
  • Environmental Monitoring and Resource Management: Our service supports environmental monitoring, land management, agriculture, forestry, mining, and other industries where detailed spectral information is critical for decision-making.

Multispectral

Survey Planning

We collaborate closely with our clients to comprehend their goals and establish the parameters for the multispectral survey. Our team of specialists develops tailored flight plans to guarantee thorough coverage of the designated area and enhance the accuracy and quality of the data collected.

We first agree the objectives, parameters and scope of the survey with the client, then prepare flight plans that give full coverage of the target area.

Flight plans take account of terrain, environmental conditions and project goals, so the data collected is reliable and meets the client's analytical needs.

Our multispectral surveys support agriculture, environmental monitoring and land use planning.

Data acquisition

Our drones carry multispectral cameras that capture high-resolution images in several spectral bands, recording visible (RGB) and near-infrared (NIR) bands together to analyse vegetation health, moisture content and other variables.

Recording RGB and near-infrared bands at the same time gives the data needed to assess vegetation condition, moisture and other factors affecting crops and the environment.

The imagery supports decisions in agriculture, forestry and natural resource management.

We keep our multispectral equipment and methods up to date so the data supports sound land management and environmental decisions.

Data Processing and Analysis

After the survey, we process the multispectral imagery with specialised software. Our analysts derive vegetation indices such as NDVI, chlorophyll levels and soil properties, and deliver reports and maps to support crop management, irrigation, pest monitoring and related work.

Typical outputs include NDVI and other vegetation index maps, chlorophyll content and soil characteristics, presented as reports and visualisations.

These outputs feed directly into planning and resource allocation in agriculture and environmental management.

Reporting

From the survey results we prepare reports aimed at improving crop yields, reducing input costs and managing risk, and we work with clients to turn the findings into practical actions.

We aim to provide dependable multispectral surveys that help clients improve productivity and manage land sustainably.

Our recommendations are based on careful analysis of the survey data and agreed with the client.
